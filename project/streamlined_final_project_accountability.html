AI Final Project

Streamlined Version with Student Accountability

Project
Important

This is an individual project.

You will have 7 to 10 days to complete it.

Please check D2L for the exact due date and submission instructions.

Goal

In this project, you will use AI to help yourself learn multiple linear regression.

The goal is not to let AI do the project for you.

The goal is to use AI as a tutor, coach, or checker while you do the statistical thinking, make predictions, verify results, and explain your own work.

What You Must Do Yourself

Some parts of this project must come from your own thinking.

You must do these parts before AI or without AI:

  1. Before AI thinking page
    Before your first AI prompt, write your own first ideas about the variables, expected signs, and possible limitations.

  2. Manual prediction task
    Use your fitted regression equation to compute and compare predicted stress values for two cases. Show your work.

  3. Brief no AI explanation
    Complete a short in person check with the instructor or submit a short one take recording in which you explain your project in your own words.

What to Submit

Submit one PDF to D2L, plus a short no AI explanation.

Your PDF should include these parts:

Part 0. Before AI Thinking

Before your first AI prompt, complete a short section of about half a page to one page.

Include:

  • the response variable and predictors
  • your predicted sign for the sleep coefficient
  • your predicted sign for the caffeine coefficient
  • which predictor you think will matter more
  • one possible limitation or concern
  • the date and time you wrote this section

Write this in your own words before using AI.

Part A. Short Data Analysis Report

Write a short report of about 2 to 3 pages.

Include:

  1. Introduction
    Briefly explain the question and define the variables.

  2. Graphs
    Include scatterplots for:

    • stress versus sleep
    • stress versus caffeine
    • sleep versus caffeine

  3. Model
    Fit a multiple linear regression model predicting stress from sleep and caffeine.

  4. Interpretation
    Interpret:

    • the intercept
    • the coefficient for sleep
    • the coefficient for caffeine
    • \(R^2\)

  5. Compare your first predictions to the fitted model
    Explain what you predicted in Part 0 and what actually happened.

  6. Manual prediction task
    Use your fitted equation to compute predicted stress for these two cases:

    • Case 1: sleep = 6 hours, caffeine = 100 mg
    • Case 2: sleep = 8 hours, caffeine = 250 mg

    Show your calculation and explain which case has higher predicted stress and why.

  7. Assumptions and limitations
    Discuss at least two assumptions or diagnostic concerns and at least one limitation.

  8. Conclusion
    Give a short conclusion in plain language.

Part B. AI Learning Record

Use the AI learning record template.

This should be about 1 page.

For your most useful AI interactions, briefly record:

  • what you were trying to learn
  • what you asked
  • what AI told you
  • what you accepted, changed, or rejected
  • how you checked it

At least one row must show you checking, correcting, or declining an AI suggestion.

Part C. Short Reflection

Write a short reflection of about 200 to 300 words.

Your reflection must answer these questions:

  • How did AI help you learn?
  • What is one thing AI explained well?
  • What is one place where AI was incomplete, vague, misleading, or wrong?
  • What is one important part you had to do by yourself?
  • How did you verify the AI output?

Brief No AI Explanation

You must also complete one of these:

Option 1. In person check

Meet briefly with the instructor and answer a few short questions about your project.

Option 2. Short recording

Submit a 2 to 3 minute one take recording.

In your recording, explain:

  • your response and predictors
  • the meaning of one coefficient
  • which case in the manual prediction task has higher predicted stress
  • one limitation of your analysis
  • one place where AI was not enough by itself

Rules for the no AI explanation:

  • no AI use while making the recording
  • no reading AI written text
  • speak in your own words

AI Rules

You may use one text based generative AI tool such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, or another approved tool.

You may use AI to

  • ask for simple explanations
  • ask AI to quiz you
  • ask for help understanding regression ideas
  • ask what assumptions you should check
  • ask AI to critique your reasoning

You may not use AI to

  • write your final report for you
  • give you final report ready paragraphs to submit
  • complete your before AI thinking page
  • do your manual prediction task for you
  • write a script for your no AI explanation
  • replace your own reasoning

A simple rule:

AI can help you learn, but it cannot replace your thinking.

Starter Prompt Options

Pick one of these to begin after you finish Part 0.

Option 1

Tip

I am learning multiple linear regression for a class project. Please teach me in simple language. Do not write my report. Ask me questions one at a time so I have to think.

Option 2

Tip

I am working on a regression project with the variables sleep, caffeine, and stress. Please act like a tutor. Help me understand the model, coefficients, assumptions, and limitations, but do not give me final report text.

Option 3

Tip

I am going to explain my reasoning about my regression project. Please tell me what is unclear, incomplete, or possibly wrong, and tell me what I should verify.

Data Set

Use the class data file health_sleep_caffeine_stress.csv provided in D2L.

Variables:

  • sleep: average hours of sleep per night
  • caffeine: average daily caffeine intake in milligrams
  • stress: perceived stress score

Use a multiple linear regression model to study how sleep and caffeine are related to stress.

Suggested Timeline

Days 1 to 2

  • complete Part 0 before AI
  • open the data
  • start your AI conversation after Part 0 is done

Days 3 to 5

  • make the graphs
  • fit the model
  • write your first interpretations
  • complete the manual prediction task

Days 6 to 8

  • ask AI to check your reasoning
  • revise your report
  • complete the AI learning record
  • write the reflection

Days 9 to 10

  • do your no AI explanation
  • combine your work into one PDF
  • submit everything

Grading

Category Points
Before AI thinking page 15
Correct statistical work and interpretation 30
Manual prediction task and comparison to first predictions 15
Clear report with graphs and model discussion 15
AI learning record and verification 10
Reflection 5
Brief no AI explanation 10

Total: 100 points

Final Reminder

A weak project uses AI to generate words.

A strong project uses AI to improve understanding, while the student still does the key thinking.
